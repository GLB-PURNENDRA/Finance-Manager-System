PROBLEM SPACE • SLIDE 02

The Problem: Family Financial Friction

Tertiary education marks the first time students handle significant living allowances, creating systemic anxiety for both parents and students.

❌ The Student Dilemma

  • ⚠️ 74% Allowance Exhaustion: Most students exhaust monthly allowances within the first 10-12 days due to untracked micro-spending.
  • ⚠️ Scattered Payment Channels: Expenses fragmented across UPI apps (GPay, PhonePe), debit cards, mess dues, and cash.
  • ⚠️ Investment Ignorance: Students treat money solely as spendable cash, missing out on compounding via SIPs and index funds.

❌ The Parental Dilemma

  • ⚠️ Zero Spending Visibility: Parents transfer funds blindly with no objective visibility into whether funds are spent on rent or impulse dining.
  • ⚠️ Invasive Interventions: Parents forced to demand net-banking passwords or screenshot proof, violating privacy and security.
  • ⚠️ Manual Toil: Commercial tools like Excel or generic expense apps require tedious daily manual logging that gets abandoned.

SECURITY MATRIX • SLIDE 05

Dual-Role RBAC & Production Hardening

Strict asymmetric permissions: eliminating demo accounts and enforcing read-only parental oversight.

👨‍🎓 STUDENT ROLE (Operator)

  • ✅ Full Ledger Control: Add, edit, and delete transactions and receipts.
  • ✅ Budget Customization: Configure monthly category caps and savings goals.
  • ✅ Investment Management: Register stocks, gold, ETFs, and mutual funds.
  • ✅ Family Linking: Generates unique code (e.g. STU-ALEX-8842) to authorize parents.

👨‍👩‍👧 PARENT ROLE (Governance)

  • 👁️ Real-Time Ledger Oversight: Inspect student transactions and spending velocity.
  • 👁️ Live Portfolio Valuation: View current market value of student assets and returns.
  • 🚫 Zero Mutation Invariant: Backend strictly throws 403 Forbidden on any edit/delete attempt.
  • 🔒 No Credential Bypass: Demo logins permanently purged; genuine JWT auth mandatory.
